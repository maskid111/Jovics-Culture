# Asset sources

## Typography and reference texture

Fraunces and Manrope font files were copied read-only from the confirmed Native reference into public/fonts. Their SIL Open Font License texts are included alongside the files.

public/images/heritage-pattern.png is an independent copy of the supplied reference texture, darkened through CSS in the same manner as Version A. It is replaceable with Jovic’s Culture artwork.

No Native logo, food imagery, video, listings or business data is used.

## Product concept illustrations

The eight SVG illustrations prefixed product- are original, code-authored concepts generated by scripts/create-product-art.mjs. They are placeholders for the sample catalogue, not official packaging, specifications or photographs. The storefront labels them as concepts and sample products.
